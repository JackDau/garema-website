Your GP in Canberra City

Garema Place Surgery has been providing health care to residents of the city and surrounding areas since 2000. Our aim is to provide you with comprehensive health care in a convenient location. Our philosophy is that General Practice is the cornerstone of health care and we believe in a holistic approach to your health, focusing not only on management of conditions but prevention.

Garema Place Surgery is now part of YourGP

We're pleased to let you know that as of 24 June 2026, Garema Place Surgery has officially joined YourGP.

Nothing changes for you as a patient. Your doctors and the team you know are staying the same, and our focus on high-quality, patient-first care continues without interruption.

Dr Felicity Donaghy will continue as the clinical lead.

Thank you for your continued trust and support as we begin this new chapter together.
