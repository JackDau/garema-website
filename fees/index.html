Fees

Garema Place Surgery is a private billing Medical Practice and payment is requested at the time of consultation. We are unable to issue accounts. Payment methods are eftpos or credit card. We do not accept cash or DVA cards.

We have Medicare Easyclaim and are able to process your Medicare refund. Fees vary according to the Medicare item number which takes into account the duration of the consultation. Procedures and Chronic Disease Management consultations are charged differently to a Standard Consultation.

A Standard Consultation is $105 (Saturday fee $115), and a Long Consultation is $185 (Saturday fee $195). A full fee list is available from Reception. If the fee differs from a Standard Consultation this will be discussed with you by your health care provider.
